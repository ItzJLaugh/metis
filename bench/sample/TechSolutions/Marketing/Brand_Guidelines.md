# Tech Solutions Brand Guidelines (v3, 2026)

## Name usage
- Company: "Tech Solutions Corporation" on first mention, "Tech Solutions" afterwards. Never "TechSol" or "TSC".
- Product: "FleetSync" (one word, capital F and S).

## Colors
- Primary: Midnight Navy #14213D
- Accent: Signal Orange #FCA311
- Neutrals: Cloud Gray #E5E5E5 and White #FFFFFF

## Typography
- Headings: Inter Bold
- Body: Inter Regular, 16px minimum on web

## Voice
Clear, confident, practical. Talk about real outcomes ("18% fewer empty miles"), not hype. Avoid "revolutionary", "disruptive" and "AI-powered" in headlines.

## Logo
Keep clear space equal to the height of the "T" on every side. Do not recolor, stretch, or place the logo on busy photos.
