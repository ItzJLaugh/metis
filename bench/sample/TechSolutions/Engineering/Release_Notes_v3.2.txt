FleetSync v3.2 — Release Notes
Released: October 28, 2026

NEW
- Route Optimization AI now considers live traffic and driver hours-of-service limits.
- Bulk load import from CSV (up to 5,000 loads per file).
- Dark mode for the customer dashboard.

IMPROVED
- Dispatch board loads 2.3x faster for fleets over 200 vehicles.
- GPS map refresh interval reduced from 30 seconds to 10 seconds.

FIXED
- Duplicate notifications when a load was reassigned twice within one minute.
- Time zone bug in weekly utilization reports for Arizona customers.

KNOWN ISSUES
- Bulk import does not yet support Excel (.xlsx) files; planned for v3.3 (December 2026).
